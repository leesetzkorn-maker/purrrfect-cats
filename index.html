KNOW THE BREED

Three classic colour groups.
Many beautiful expressions.

Bengal colours and patterns can vary widely. TICA lists Brown Tabby, Silver Tabby and Snow varieties including Seal Lynx Point, Seal Mink and Seal Sepia, with spotted and marble patterns among the recognised Bengal expressions. citeturn0search0turn0search16

01

Brown

Rich warm backgrounds with high-contrast dark markings. Rosetted brown Bengals create the classic mini-leopard look.

02

Snow

Snow Bengals include Seal Lynx Point, Seal Mink and Seal Sepia varieties, with eye colour varying by genetic type.

03

Silver

Cool silver backgrounds with contrasting dark markings, available in spotted and marble expressions.

04

Marble

A flowing pattern rather than traditional spots. Marble is a recognised Bengal pattern alongside spotted.

Active minds. Athletic bodies.Bengals are known for being intelligent, curious, active and engaging — they often thrive with play, enrichment and interaction. citeturn0search5turn0search0

QUESTIONS

Before you
bring one home.

Start with the questions that matter most.

Are Bengals suitable for families?

They can be, provided the family understands their active, intelligent and social nature. The individual cat and your household both matter.

Can I choose a kitten by colour only?

Colour and markings can be discussed, but personality and lifestyle compatibility should remain part of the final match.

How long do Bengals live?

TICA gives a Bengal life expectancy of roughly 12 to 20 years depending on factors including environment and nutrition. citeturn0search0

Does joining the waitlist guarantee a kitten?

No. The waitlist starts a conversation. Availability and final matching are confirmed separately.

Can I travel with my kitten?

Travel can require veterinary preparation and destination-specific documentation. Requirements should be checked for the exact destination before arrangements are made.

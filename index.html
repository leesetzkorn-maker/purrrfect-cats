OUR STORY

It started with
Soephia.

Sonja has always shared her home with cats, from pavement specials to Siamese and Abyssinians. Then Soephia arrived and the Bengal breed found a special place in her heart.

Soephia and Sylvester made the journey with Sonja to their forever home in Cape Town. Soephia quickly became the household character — playing fetch, collecting toys and ruling her den.

Her first litter brought a tiny white Snow Bengal, the much-loved Minkie. Later, Sterlinkie joined the family with exotic Russian bloodlines, bringing remarkable rosettes and silver tinsel into the story.

“They are so curious and adventurous!”
